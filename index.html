Poison Check

v3Spots images that may have hidden AI-poisoning (like Nightshade or Glaze). Everything runs on your device: your images are never uploaded.

1. Pick the images to check

Pick 8 or more if you can. The checker compares images against each other to find the odd ones out.

If photos show "couldn't be read", use Choose from Files instead.

How to read the results

Looks normal: nothing stood out.

Worth a closer look: one warning sign. Zoom in and check smooth areas (sky, skin, plain backgrounds) for faint swirly texture.

Suspicious: several warning signs at once.

Grainy photos, heavily compressed JPEGs and noisy art styles can trigger false alarms, so treat this as a "look here first" list, not proof.

The checks: fine texture hiding in smooth areas; unusual amounts of very fine detail; how much the AI's view of the image shifts after a light clean-up (poisoning tends to break when you do that); and, if you add originals, how many pixels were changed.
